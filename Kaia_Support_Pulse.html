Kaia Support Pulse

Source: #kaia-questions Slack channel Window: May 20 – Sep 17, 2026 (120 days) 309 tickets classified Prepared Sep 21, 2026

Over the last 120 days, #kaia-questions generated 309 distinct support threads. The headline: this is not an onboarding channel — 60% of volume sits in Production/Steady-State, meaning most tickets come from customers and internal teams already live on Kaia hitting recording, consent, and admin friction day to day. Meeting Recording/Transcription and Data Privacy & Recording Consent are the two largest problem areas, and three issues this window were significant enough to be flagged Critical. Enterprise customers and internal (sales/dogfood) traffic are split almost exactly down the middle.

Methodology & caveats
• Each Slack thread (parent message + all replies) in #kaia-questions was treated as one ticket; 18 non-substantive fragments (empty messages, "bumping" duplicates, one-word replies) were excluded from counts. • Requester role/function is based on verified Slack profile titles where available; ambiguous titles are marked "inferred" in the underlying data. • Priority (Critical/High/Medium/Low) is inferred from urgency language in the thread — it is an analytical estimate, not a real support-queue SLA field. • Window covers the most recent 120 days (May 20 – Sep 17, 2026), not a full year — a fuller trailing-12-month pull can be run as a follow-up. • Full 309-row ticket detail, plus Trends / Phase Breakdown / Requesters tabs, is in the companion workbook Kaia_Support_Tickets_120d.xlsx.
